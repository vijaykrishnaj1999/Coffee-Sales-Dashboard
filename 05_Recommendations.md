# Business Recommendations

The following recommendations are based on the analyses provided by the dashboard.

---

## 1. Focus on High-Performing Products

Identify products that consistently generate the highest revenue and ensure they remain well stocked.

Consider promotional campaigns that feature these products to maximize sales.

---

## 2. Improve Underperforming Products

Review products with consistently low sales.

Possible actions include:

- Updating pricing
- Offering promotional discounts
- Replacing low-performing items
- Improving product visibility

---

## 3. Optimize Store Performance

Compare store performance regularly.

Investigate operational differences between high-performing and low-performing locations to identify opportunities for improvement.

---

## 4. Improve Staffing During Peak Hours

Use hourly sales patterns to schedule employees more effectively.

Increasing staff during busy periods can improve customer service while reducing wait times.

---

## 5. Monitor Sales Trends

Track monthly and seasonal sales trends to support demand forecasting and inventory planning.

---

## 6. Build a Data-Driven Decision Process

Use the dashboard as a regular management reporting tool.

Monitoring KPIs consistently enables faster responses to changing business conditions and supports strategic decision-making.

---

# Conclusion

This dashboard provides management with a centralized view of sales performance across stores, products, and time.

By transforming transactional data into interactive visualizations, the report supports operational improvements and informed business decisions.
