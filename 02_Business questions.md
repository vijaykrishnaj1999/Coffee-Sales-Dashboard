# Business Questions

The dashboard is designed to answer the following business questions.

---

## 1. How is the business performing overall?

Monitor the overall health of the business using key performance indicators such as:

- Total Revenue
- Total Transactions
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value

---

## 2. How have sales changed over time?

Analyze sales trends to determine whether business performance is improving or declining.

This includes:

- Daily Sales Trend
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Revenue Growth

---

## 3. Which store locations perform the best?

Compare store performance to identify top-performing and underperforming locations.

Metrics include:

- Revenue by Store
- Transactions by Store
- Average Order Value by Store

---

## 4. Which products contribute the most to revenue?

Evaluate product performance to identify products and categories that generate the highest sales.

Metrics include:

- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Product
- Top Selling Products
- Lowest Performing Products

---

## 5. When do customers purchase the most?

Understand customer purchasing behavior by analyzing sales throughout the day and week.

Metrics include:

- Sales by Hour
- Sales by Day of Week
- Peak Business Hours

---

## Expected Outcome

The insights generated from this dashboard will help management improve sales performance, optimize operations, and make informed business decisions.
