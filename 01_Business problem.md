# Business Problem

## Background

The coffee shop chain operates multiple store locations and records thousands of daily sales transactions. Although the business collects transactional data, management lacks a centralized reporting system to monitor sales performance and support decision-making.

Without an interactive dashboard, it is difficult to answer important business questions such as:

- Are sales improving over time?
- Which stores generate the highest revenue?
- Which products contribute the most to sales?
- When are customers most active?

As a result, business decisions are often based on assumptions instead of data.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables management to monitor sales performance, compare store locations, evaluate product performance, and identify customer purchasing trends.

The dashboard provides decision-makers with real-time insights that support operational and strategic planning.

---

## Business Goals

This project aims to help management:

- Monitor overall business performance using key performance indicators.
- Track sales trends over time.
- Compare the performance of different store locations.
- Identify the best-selling and lowest-performing products.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior throughout the day and week.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Expected Business Value

The dashboard enables management to:

- Increase revenue by focusing on high-performing products.
- Improve inventory planning.
- Optimize staffing during peak business hours.
- Identify underperforming stores that require attention.
- Make faster and more informed business decisions.
